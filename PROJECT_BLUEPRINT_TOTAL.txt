14) Fiscal Readiness Mini Checklist (MX)
Goal:
Improve collection strength, trust, and scaling readiness without blocking immediate execution.

Checklist:
1. Confirm current SAT status (regimen, obligations, CFDI scope).
2. Validate if migration to Persona Fisica con Actividad Empresarial is applicable.
3. Confirm invoicing implications and billing obligations after migration.
4. Open/upgrade PayPal business profile aligned with fiscal data.
5. Open/confirm business-oriented bank account for clearer reconciliation.
6. Align account holder/legal names across SAT, bank, and payment providers.
7. Update checkout legal text and payment evidence workflow.

Advantages of continuing WITHOUT fiscal/business upgrade (short term):
1. Faster immediate start and lower setup friction.
2. Quicker validation of demand and conversion assumptions.
3. Lower cognitive overhead in the first launch window.

Disadvantages of continuing WITHOUT fiscal/business upgrade:
1. Lower trust for B2B buyers and higher objection rate at checkout.
2. More reconciliation friction and weaker accounting clarity.
3. Higher risk of operational bottlenecks when volume increases.
4. Less flexibility for formal invoicing and enterprise-style sales.
