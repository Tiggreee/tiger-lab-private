# Funnel Recovery Playbook

## Trigger
- Conversion from visit to checkout falls below baseline.

## Immediate Actions
1. Confirm traffic source quality by channel.
2. Run diagnostic simulators for funnel and traffic.
3. Verify pricing resolver output for top plans.
4. Review alert stream for correlated anomalies.

## Stabilization
- Route low-quality channels to education sequence.
- Increase lead scoring refresh cadence.
- Temporarily prioritize high-intent handoff.

## Exit Criteria
- Visit to checkout conversion returns above minimum threshold.
